# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone participating in this project.

Participants should use welcoming language, respect differing viewpoints,
accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the community, and
show empathy toward others.

Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, publishing another person's private
information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents
the project. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
code.

Report unacceptable behavior to `GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com`. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and handled as appropriate to the circumstances.
